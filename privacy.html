Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

This policy covers the Genius American English website and the "Genius American English Uploader" app (the "App"), a private tool the channel owner uses to upload lesson videos to the Genius American English YouTube channel.

Who uses the App

The App is used only by the owner of the Genius American English channel, to manage that channel. It is not offered to the public, and viewers never sign in to it.

What Google data the App accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the App asks for these YouTube permissions:

  • youtube.upload: to upload the channel's own lesson videos.
  • youtube.force-ssl: to add captions, titles, descriptions and thumbnails to those videos and to check their upload status.

The App does not read, collect or store viewers' personal information, comments, subscriber lists, watch history, email, contacts or any other Google account data.

How data is stored

The sign-in token Google issues is stored only on the channel owner's own computer, in a file readable only by that user. It is not sent to any server operated by us or anyone else. Videos and captions are sent directly from that computer to YouTube.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share Google user data with anyone. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not transfer it to third parties. Data obtained through Google APIs is used only to provide the upload features described above.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The App uses YouTube API Services; see also the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the token file on the owner's computer also removes the App's access.

This website

This website has no sign-in, no forms, no cookies and no analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) under GitHub's privacy statement.

YouTube viewers

If you watch our videos on YouTube, YouTube's own privacy policy applies. We only see the summary statistics YouTube shows every channel owner.

Children

The App is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone other than the channel owner.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions: use the contact details on our YouTube channel About page.
